I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/graduates/instream-students), currently on the 2025–2026 job market.

My research lies at the intersection of **Labour Economics and Macroeconomics**.
Most of my [work](/publications/) uses Canadian administrative microdata to study **labour market power**,
the **distributional effects of macroeconomic shocks**, and the **fiscal policy** responses designed to mitigate them.
My thesis, *Three Essays in Labour and Macroeconomics*, is advised by [Fabian Lange](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/fabian-lange) and [Markus Poschke](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/markus-poschke).
