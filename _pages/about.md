I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at [McGill University](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/graduates/instream-students) (thesis submitted May 2026) and a researcher at CIREQ.

My research lies at the intersection of **Labour Economics and Macroeconomics**.
Most of my [work](/publications/) uses Canadian administrative microdata and structural macroeconomic models to study **labour market power**,
the **distributional effects of macroeconomic shocks**, and the **monetary and fiscal policy** responses designed to mitigate them.
My thesis, *Three Essays in Labour and Macroeconomics*, is supervised by [Fabian Lange](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/fabian-lange) and co-supervised by [Markus Poschke](https://www.mcgill.ca/economics/markus-poschke).
